IT-BPM fresher hiring fell from 4 lakh to 1.75 lakh jobs in 4 years. The recovery is real, but it's not for the same resume.

A full breakdown of HAN Digital's IT-BPM Graduate Hiring Outlook 2027, reported by the Economic Times on October 14, 2026, mapped against which roles, cities, and skills are actually growing right now.

Where the new jobs actually are

  • Growing: Data engineering, AI testing and validation, annotation, model operations, specialised technology roles (AI, cybersecurity).
  • Shrinking: Bulk routine coding, conventional entry-level BPO execution work.

What this means for your prep

The old hiring model, bulk hiring followed by six months of generic training, is structurally changing. Companies are no longer just screening for a CS degree and basic coding ability; they are testing directly for whether a candidate can work with AI systems, validate outputs, and apply that to a real business problem. If your interview prep is aimed purely at conventional DSA and services-company drives, you're preparing for the shrinking half of this market. If it includes data engineering, AI/ML fundamentals, or GCC-specific assessments, you're preparing for the half that's actually growing.
